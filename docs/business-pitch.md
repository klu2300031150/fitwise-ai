# Business Pitch

FitWise AI helps apparel sellers reduce sizing friction and returns by generating fit-aware charts from existing product assets and by recommending the best size to each customer.

Why it wins:

- It turns fragmented product data into structured size intelligence.
- It improves customer confidence by explaining why a size was recommended.
- It uses low-latency heuristics and caching to stay practical for storefront use.
- It is privacy-safe because it never stores raw customer body photos.

The MVP is positioned as a seller-facing sizing layer that can plug into any commerce stack.
